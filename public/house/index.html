One gate, two cars

5.2 cents, slanted on the north

11 m on the lane and 16 m on the 3.7 m internal road, with the north edge slanting out to a back edge of about 15.3 m. Two open courtyards run out to the boundary walls.

03 · The heart

A garden under the stair

The stair floats over a pebble garden beside the slanted wall, lit by a skylight two floors up. A steel grille, not glass, opens it to a north courtyard that runs to the boundary wall.

04 · Ground floor · 1,100 sq.ft

Ground floor

A sunken living room at the door with the prayer wall straight ahead, the kitchen in the north-east corner beside the well, and two bedroom suites. The master opens to its own courtyard.

05 · First floor · 950 sq.ft

Upstairs

Two bedroom suites upstairs: Bedroom 3 (about 21 m²) in front and Bedroom 4 (about 18 m²) at the back, each with a walk-in closet that leads through to its own bath, a 55-inch TV and a balcony. The theatre is gone; the landing stays open.

06 · The roof works

Solar, tank and a way up

The stair continues to a roof cabin with the 2,000 L tank on top. A raised steel frame carries about 8.8 kW of solar panels, enough for the house by day and to charge a car, with shaded roof space underneath.

4 m from the lane (3 m required, plus 1 m for parking), 2 m from the internal road, and 1.5 m on the north side and at the back. The courtyards in those yards stay open to the sky.

    About the courtyards in the yards

    You said the courtyards can run to the boundary walls outside the permit. As drawn they don't need one: they are open to the sky, with paving, plants and 2.1 m boundary walls, which open yards allow. A roof or room built out into those yards would be unauthorised and can hold up the occupancy certificate, so every roofed part of the house stays inside the setback lines.

    Shape

    5.2 cents is 210.4 m². Keeping 11.00 m on the lane and 16.00 m on the internal road, square to each other, the back edge works out to about 15.3 m and the north edge to about 16.6 m. Your earlier sketch said 15.8 m for the back; if that holds, one corner isn't square, and the survey sketch will show which.

    Facing

    The front faces due west onto the lane. The internal road runs along the south side, and the slanted north edge faces the neighbours.

    Roads

    The lane is about 3.5 m of interlocking pavers with a low kerb along your plot. The internal road is 3.7 m wide and runs about 36 m east. If either is an unnotified road under 6 m wide, the 2026 rule sets the yard beside it at 2 m.

    Sun

    At 10.2° N the sun is straight overhead around 17 April and 25 August. It passes north from May to August and south the rest of the year. The tall west glass gets a clay jaali screen against the afternoon sun.

    Rain and levels

    The plot is level with the lane. The plinth sits at +0.60 m, and the parking strip falls toward the gate. Roof water goes to a sump and a recharge pit, with overflow to the well. Ask neighbours about the 2018 flood level here.

    Neighbours

    A white shed with a striped metal roof sits just past the north boundary by the lane, and a two-storey house is under construction beyond it. Open field with one tree clump lies to the east, houses stand 30–50 m to the north-east and east, and a red-tiled house sits across the lane to the south-west. Palms and dense trees fill the rest.

    Airport

    Cochin International Airport is about 8 km south. The tank on the stair cabin tops out at about 11 m, well below the usual limits, but check the AAI colour-coded zoning map.

    Ground

    Order a soil test before structural design. Lateritic soils are common here and usually carry a two-storey house on shallow footings.

    E-01Budget

    ₹35 lakh: what fits, and what comes later

    At 2026 Kochi rates, your full wish list costs more than ₹35 lakh. On a budget specification the house prices at about ₹35–38 L on a budget specification, so reaching ₹35 L needs the savings listed under Phase 1. The plan is to build Phase 1 first, with conduits, roof frame bases and electrical capacity for everything else already in place, so the rest plugs in later.

    Phase 1, ₹35 L with savings

    1. The whole house, with the upstairs open space left plain: tiled floor, painted walls and lights.
    2. Savings to close the last ₹1–3 L: keep the existing lane wall, use 600 × 600 tile in the bedrooms, leave the west jaali screen and the balcony planter for later, buy sanitaryware and fittings directly, and lock steel and cement rates in the contract.
    3. Budget specification: texture paint, vitrified tile, UPVC windows, an RCC stair with wood-look tile, MS railings and gates, and one mid-range sanitaryware range.
    4. Pre-wiring now: solar conduits and DC cable route, a 32 A EV circuit to the front wall, CCTV and data cables to nine camera points.
    5. Basic kitchen cabinets. Wardrobes and furniture come after moving in.

    Phase 2, when funds allow

    1. Solar, about 8.8 kW, with a hybrid inverter. Roughly ₹5–6 L. PM Surya Ghar pays a subsidy of up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above; check the current terms with KSEB.
    2. Battery, 10 kWh, for power cuts: about ₹3–4 L.
    3. EV wallbox charger (7.4 kW): about ₹0.4–0.7 L.
    4. CCTV: nine cameras, an NVR and an Apple TV as the HomeKit hub, about ₹0.7–1.0 L, plus a cloud plan of about ₹1,000–2,500 a month.
    Where Phase 1's ₹35 lakh goesTypical share₹ lakh

    House rates: Infralens puts a basic Kochi build at ₹1,995–2,415 per sq.ft and standard at ₹2,125–2,753 (May 2026). Kerala Kaumudi reported about ₹2,500 per sq.ft statewide (Aug 2026). One contractor package in your references advertises ₹1,867 per sq.ft without interiors. The solar, battery, EV and CCTV figures are rough market ranges for planning; get quotes. The shares are typical for a Kerala RCC house; your bill of quantities sets the real numbers.

    From this concept to a permit and a site

    1. Lock the site facts

      Get the survey sketch, which confirms the 5.2 cents and the back edge, and a soil test. Check the paddy and wetland data bank. Note the position of any wells. Ask whether the lane and the internal road are notified roads.

      This month

    2. Design development

      Walk the family through this model, confirm the prayer wall, porch and carport, and fix the structural grid. That includes the cantilever over the porch and the solar frame bases.

      2–4 weeks

    3. Bill of quantities and quotes

      Price Phase 1 with three contractors before the permit, so the ₹35 L test happens early.

      3–4 weeks

    4. Permit drawings

      A registered licensee prepares the drawings and files them on K-SMART. If the house qualifies as low-risk, the permit can be self-certified.

      1–4 weeks

    5. Working drawings

      Structure for seismic zone III, plumbing, the electrical layout from this services plan, rainwater and septic.

      3–4 weeks

    6. Build

      Start after the north-east monsoon (December–January) so the roof is cast before June.

      10–14 months

    Q-01Questions

    What I need from you

    1. Survey sketch. At 5.2 cents with 11 m and 16 m frontages, the back edge is about 15.3 m; your earlier sketch said 15.8 m. The sketch decides which corner gives.
    2. Road status. Are the lane and the internal road notified roads? It changes the yard rules beside them.
    3. Carport canopy. Ask your licensee whether the panchayat accepts an open car porch in the front yard. If not, the canopy can shrink back to the 1 m in front of the house.
    4. Open space upstairs. Leaving part of it as a roofed terrace without walls would cut the cost. Shall I price that?
    5. Wells next door. Are there wells on the neighbouring plots? The septic tank must be 7.5 m from them too.
